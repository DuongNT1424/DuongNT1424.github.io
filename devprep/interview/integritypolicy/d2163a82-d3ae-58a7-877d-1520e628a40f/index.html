Interview Integrity Policy

To ensure a fair and secure assessment environment, please review the following rules before beginning your session.

videocam

Video Visibility

Your camera must be turned on and your face clearly visible throughout the entire session.

person

Single Participant

You must be the only person in the room. No other individuals may be present or assist you.

block

No Assistance

Use of external resources, AI tools, or secondary devices is strictly prohibited.

record_voice_over

Voice Verification

When you answer by voice, your speech will be verified against your enrolled voice sample. Using AI-generated voices or external audio sources is strictly prohibited.

timer

Session Timer

The session timer runs continuously once started and is not paused by a lost connection, closed browser, or technical issue. Use the Pause button if you need a break — pauses are limited in count and total duration, and any credit already used is non-refundable.

warning

Violation Policy

The assessment will automatically terminate upon reaching a maximum of 3 recorded violations.

visibility

AI Monitoring Active

This session utilizes advanced AI monitoring to ensure compliance with the integrity policy. Data is securely processed and reviewed.
